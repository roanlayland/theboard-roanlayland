Garrett Wilson — 2026 Fantasy Football Outlook

WR · Jets · Bye 13 · ADP 36.5

One of the most talented receivers of his draft class, Wilson has spent his entire career producing despite catastrophic quarterback play — which is itself the strongest possible argument for his talent. His fantasy value has always been suppressed by his environment rather than his ability. He is the classic buy-low profile with a very high ceiling if the situation ever improves.
